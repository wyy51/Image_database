Product Description

Cross-arm (scissor-type) regulator ensures stable and powerful window lifting, with strong load-bearing capacity. It effectively helps reduce glass jamming and deviation during use.

Includes pre-installed electric motor, so there is no need for additional purchase or separate assembly. This saves installation time and reduces maintenance cost, making replacement more efficient.

Quiet and powerful motor design offers low operating noise, sufficient output power and responsive performance, helping ensure smooth window operation and longer service life.

Fully tested before leaving the factory, with inspection focused on lifting smoothness, structural durability and motor performance, helping reduce the risk of defective installation.

Direct fit replacement that is designed to match the original factory mounting points accurately. No drilling, cutting or other modifications are required under normal installation conditions.

Ideal replacement for common failure situations such as damaged original regulator assemblies, slow lifting, jamming, abnormal noise, unstable movement or complete failure.
